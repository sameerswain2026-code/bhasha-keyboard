Delete your Bhasha Aura account

You can permanently delete your account directly in the Android companion app.

  1. Open Bhasha Aura.
  2. On the dashboard, open the menu on your connected account card.
  3. Choose Delete cloud account.
  4. Review the warning and choose Delete permanently.

What is deleted

  • Your Bhasha/Appwrite account and active sessions.
  • Linked-document metadata owned by your account.
  • The encrypted Google Drive refresh token held by the Bhasha backend.

What is not deleted

Files stored in your Google Drive are not deleted. Local keyboard settings remain on the device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

If the in-app deletion action cannot be completed, do not post private account information in a public issue. Use the repository's private security-advisory contact for sensitive requests.
